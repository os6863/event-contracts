FROM SOURCE TO SIGNAL

Evidence at every step.

Somnia × DreamDEX

Two on-chain agents. One independent baseline. A transparent comparison you can inspect.

01↗

DreamDEX market

Read the opening price, order-book probability and on-chain trading status.

02↗

Somnia price agent

Fetch BTC / ETH spot data through consensus-validated agent execution.

03↗

LLM estimator

Estimate YES probability from the timestamped inputs. Check the final response against its receipt.

04↗

Ensemble gate

Average the two estimates. Strong requires both to cross the 15-point threshold in the same direction.

The baseline uses a normal CDF with √time scaling and assumed annual volatility: BTC 55%, ETH 70%. These experimental models are not calibrated financial forecasts.
